# Adopters

Organizations and projects using credential-badges in production, in a pilot,
or as a dependency. Foundations ask for this list, and it helps new adopters
find someone who has done it before.

To add yourself, open a PR that adds a row. Keep the description to one line
and link to something public if you can.

| Organization | Status | Evidence source / adapter | How it's used | Since |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Andamio](https://andamio.io) | Production | Cardano / Andamio Course V2 (reference adapter) | Issues signed OB 3.0 credentials and badges for on-chain course credentials at `credentials.andamio.io`. Badges appear in the Andamio app, on share pages, and through the `@andamio/andamio-badge` web component. | 2026-06 (v1.0 mainnet core) |

**Status** is one of *Production*, *Pilot*, *Evaluating*, or *Integration*
(the project consumes or displays these credentials without issuing them).

## Course owners issuing through Andamio

Organizations whose courses issue credentials through Andamio's deployment are
adopters of Andamio rather than independent deployments. They are welcome here
as *Integration* rows if they want to be listed.

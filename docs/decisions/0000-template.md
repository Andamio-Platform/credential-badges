# NNNN. Title in the imperative

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** the maintainers who approved it
- **Vote:** if a recorded vote was required (see GOVERNANCE.md): result and link

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem does this solve, and why now? Link the
issue, the incident, or the `docs/solutions/` entry that prompted it.

## Decision

change follows it or breaks it.

## Options considered

The alternatives, and why each was not chosen.

## Consequences

(invariants, tests, CODEOWNERS gates, published artifacts).
